01

RAW-first import

RAW 优先导入

Work with supported camera RAW formats and standard photos while preserving the originals. Matching profiles can correct geometric lens distortion; automatic vignetting and chromatic-aberration correction are not currently included.

导入受支持的相机 RAW 与标准照片，不修改原始文件。配置匹配时可校正镜头几何畸变；目前不包含自动暗角和色差校正。

02

Intelligent stacking

智能照片堆栈

Align and combine frames for cleaner detail, smoother motion, light trails, or unwanted-object cleanup.

自动对齐并合成多帧，用于降噪、柔化运动、光轨与画面清理。

03

On-device AI denoise

设备端 AI 降噪

NAFNet-based enhancement reduces visible noise locally, with a non-AI fallback when needed.

基于 NAFNet 的增强在本地减少噪点，并在需要时自动使用非 AI 方案。

04

AI super-resolution

AI 超分辨率

Real-ESRGAN 4× enhancement can recover perceived detail for compatible images and devices.

Real-ESRGAN 4× 增强可在兼容图像和设备上提升观感细节。

05

Precise finishing tools

精细调色工具

在统一工作流中调整曝光、影调、色彩、清晰度、锐化、降噪与裁切。
